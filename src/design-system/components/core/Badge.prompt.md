Small status / category label. Always pairs a tone with a word (color is never the only signal).

```jsx
<Badge tone="success" dot>Reconciled</Badge>
<Badge tone="warning" dot>Needs owner</Badge>
<Badge tone="info">Imported</Badge>
<Badge tone="primary" square>AI</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral · success · warning · danger · info · primary`. `dot` adds a leading status dot; `square` switches to xs-radius for counts/codes.
